## What I do

- Audit document hierarchies to ensure they match formal academic or thesis structures.
- Format complex datasets into clean, accessible Markdown or LaTeX data tables.
- Standardize technical report tones, ensuring clear transitions between literature reviews, methodology, and results.

## When to use me

Use this skill when initializing structural drafts for a report, checking the flow of a multi-page chapter, or converting raw project notes into structured report summaries.
